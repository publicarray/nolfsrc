# T01S02-bottle.txt -- a scene for build/playtest.sh (make test-renderers):
# T01S02's opening cinematic, "Residence of Cate Archer", as the camera pans
# along the bathroom shelf past the perfume, the wine bottle and the glass.
# d3d9.ren and opengl.ren drew the models' back faces, and the chrome
# passes translucent (the bottle and the glass see-through), where d3d7.ren
# (DirectX 7) does not. Timed for the modern build, which plays the cinematic at its
# speed (the bathroom part about 45 s, as checked on Windows). The legacy
# build, like the retail game, plays it faster the higher its frame rate:
# about 18 s under Xvfb, 7 s on Windows with a fast GPU; its shots at these
# times are of other parts of it.
window 240
cinestart 120
# the shelf from about 18s in, the bottle and the glass at 22-30s
wait 18
shot shelf-18s
wait 2
shot shelf-20s
wait 2
shot shelf-22s
wait 2
shot bottle-24s
wait 2
shot bottle-26s
wait 2
shot glass-28s
wait 2
shot glass-30s
wait 2
shot shelf-32s
