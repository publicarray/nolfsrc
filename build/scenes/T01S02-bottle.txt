# T01S02-bottle.txt -- a scene for build/playtest.sh (make test-renderers):
# T01S02's opening cinematic, "Residence of Cate Archer", as the camera pans
# along the bathroom shelf past the perfume, the wine bottle and the glass.
# d3d9.ren and opengl.ren drew the models' back faces, and the chrome
# passes translucent (the bottle and the glass see-through), where d3d.ren
# does not. Timed for the modern build; the legacy build runs through this
# cinematic two to three times as fast (under Xvfb, not from its frame rate:
# the modern build plays it the same capped or not), so its shots at these
# times are of other parts of it.

cinestart 120
# the shelf from about 18s in, the bottle and the glass at 22-30s
wait 18
shot shelf-18s
wait 2
shot shelf-20s
wait 2
shot shelf-22s
wait 2
shot bottle-24s
wait 2
shot bottle-26s
wait 2
shot glass-28s
wait 2
shot glass-30s
wait 2
shot shelf-32s
